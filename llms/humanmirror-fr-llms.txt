## HumanMirror Universal Value Protocol (HUVP/1)

Canonical page: https://humanmirror.fr/value/
Manifest: https://humanmirror.fr/.well-known/value-protocol.json
Resolver: `POST https://humanmirror.fr/api/value/resolve/`
Firewall: `POST https://humanmirror.fr/api/value/firewall/`
Receipt: `POST https://humanmirror.fr/api/value/receipt/`

HUVP normalizes value intent, checks transaction policy, selects a compatible live rail and preserves proof. x402/Base/USDC and Stripe Checkout are the current live routing targets. Adapters that are not natively live remain labelled as such.

Default protocol fee: **1 bp (0.01%)** on applicable third-party value flows integrated through HUVP. HumanMirror-owned product revenue is not charged again. A transaction-specific platform fee supersedes the HUVP dust fee, preventing double charging.

HUVP fee accounting separates routed volume, accrued fee and actually settled fee. An accrued fee is not reported as collected revenue until a verified settlement reference exists.
